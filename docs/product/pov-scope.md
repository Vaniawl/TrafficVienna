# Point-of-View Scope

TrafficVienna's current point of view is a focused, account-free companion for Vienna
public transport. Home, Discover, Alerts, and Saved form the primary journeys. The app
may use fixed public transport data and local Apple system surfaces while keeping its
existing native architecture and privacy boundary.

The active requirements and definition of done are in `SPEC.md`. Product behavior and
historical decisions remain in `PROJECT.md`, `STATUS.md`, `DECISIONS.md`, and `memory/`.
Agent-framework adoption is tooling-only and does not expand this scope.
